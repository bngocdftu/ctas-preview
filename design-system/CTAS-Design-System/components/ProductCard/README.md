# ProductCard
A technology product on the maroon products band, with a short description, three points and two links.

- Place it on a `maroon` surface (the `.ct-band` wrapper in the preview); it is not designed for cream.
- Consumer provides `name`, `domain` (the product's host, shown small above the name), `description` (one or two sentences), `points` (up to three strings), `learnHref` (the live product; opens in a new tab as "Tìm hiểu thêm ↗") and `introHref` (the article that explains it, "Giới thiệu").
- `image` is optional. `highlight` fills the card `blush`; use it for the middle card of three only.
